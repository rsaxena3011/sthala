The fort played a crucial role in repelling a Portuguese attack in 1772, demonstrating its continued importance. [History] [History]

The fort’s architecture is defined by its defensive design, featuring a high stone wall and a pentagonal room with a long domed corridor. [History] [History]
Its strategic location, atop a sheer cliff overlooking the Sewree mudflats, now attracts migratory birds, particularly flamingos. [History] [History]
